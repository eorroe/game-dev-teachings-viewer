## Glossary / Index

|Term|Definition|
|----|----------|
|Animation|The process of creating motion and movement within a 3D scene|
|Camera Work|The art of framing shots and controlling camera movement in a 3D scene to achieve cinematic results|
|Decals|Textures projected onto surfaces in a 3D scene to add details like signage or damage|
|Lighting|The setup and configuration of light sources in a 3D scene to achieve desired mood and realism|
|Lumen|Unreal Engine 5's real-time global illumination and reflections system|
|Materials|Surface properties (color, texture, reflectivity) applied to 3D objects|
|Modeling|The process of creating 3D objects and geometry|
|Motion Graphics|Animated graphic design elements created for film, television, or digital media|
|Photogrammetry|The technique of creating 3D models from photographs of real-world objects and environments|
|Polycam|A photogrammetry application used to scan real-life assets for use in 3D software like Unreal Engine 5|
|Post Processing|Visual effects applied after rendering to enhance the final image|
|Render Settings|Configuration options that control how a 3D scene is exported as an image or video|
|Star Wars Spaceship Hangar|The flagship project built throughout this tutorial series, featuring a complete 3D environment and cinematic animation|
|Unreal Engine 5 (UE5)|A free, powerful, industry-standard 3D software platform known for photorealistic real-time graphics|
|Unreal Library|Unreal Engine 5's built-in asset library containing models, materials, and other resources available for use in projects|
